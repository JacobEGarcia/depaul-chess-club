First-timer or rated player, pull up a chair.

We meet every Thursday evening in Arts and Letters Hall room 412. Boards, pieces, and clocks are provided - you bring nothing but yourself.

Casual games, blitz, puzzles, and the occasional internal tournament. Never touched a board? Someone will teach you in one meeting.

Come once or come every week. No dues, no sign-up, no tryouts.
